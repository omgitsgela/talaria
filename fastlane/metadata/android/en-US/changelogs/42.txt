Conversations are now grouped by where they came from. Your own conversations stay in the list, while cron jobs, subagent runs, Telegram and API sessions are held behind a filter that shows how many are waiting and reveals them with one tap, grouped and labelled. Pinning a session always shows it. Only sources recognised as non-human are ever hidden, so an unknown platform stays visible. Also fixes the version the app reported, which had been showing 1.2.6 since build 37.
