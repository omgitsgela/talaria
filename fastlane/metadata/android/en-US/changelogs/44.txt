Approvals and the sudo password prompt now appear in the app, so you can authorize things from your phone: approvals show the command with allow-once, allow-session, always-allow and deny, and passwords are entered in a masked field that goes straight to the gateway. /yolo now shows whether approvals are actually being skipped. Attaching a file no longer fails silently: it recovers from a stale session, confirms what was attached, explains a failure, and refuses a file too large for a single frame with a clear message.
